How it learns

Each test is a new universe: random laws or a mutation of an interesting one. It runs without drawing and is described by general numbers only. Score = viability × (novelty + persistence + enclosures + elements): not a gas, not one lump, unlike what was seen before, and producing many different stable sizes – our elements. Nothing tells it which shape to build.
